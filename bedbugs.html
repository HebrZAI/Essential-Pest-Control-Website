Why the supermarket spray isn't working

Because bedbugs are built to sit tight and outlast a single spray.

Bedbugs feed at night and spend the rest of their time flattened into mattress seams, bed frame joints, skirting gaps and even behind picture frames — places a can of surface spray never actually reaches. Their eggs are also resistant to most contact insecticides, so a treatment that only kills the bugs you can see leaves the next generation to hatch out days later, which is why DIY attempts so often look like they've worked and then flare up again. Our approach combines a thorough room-by-room inspection with targeted crack-and-crevice residual treatment at every activity point, backed by a scheduled follow-up visit to catch anything that hatches after the first round.

Worth checking for

Signs You've Got More Than a Bad Night's Sleep

Bites in a line or cluster

Waking up with several bites in a row on exposed skin is a classic bedbug pattern, since they feed repeatedly along a blood vessel.

Small rust-coloured spots on bedding

These are crushed blood spots or droppings left along seams, piping and the underside of the mattress.

A musty, sweetish smell

A heavier infestation often gives off a faint sweet odour from the bugs' scent glands, noticeable near the bed.

Shed skins or tiny eggs in seams

Pale, papery skins and tiny pale eggs tucked into mattress seams or frame joints point to an active, breeding population.

Good to know

Bedbug Treatment FAQs

Is the treatment safe for kids and pets?

Yes — we use products and application methods that are safe for occupied homes with children and pets, and we'll always tell you if any brief precaution is needed for a specific product.

How soon will I see results?

Most households notice far fewer bites within the first day, but because eggs can survive the first treatment, full resolution usually takes two treatments over roughly 3–4 weeks.

Will one treatment be enough?

Rarely on its own — bedbug eggs are naturally resistant to most treatments, so we build in a follow-up visit as standard rather than waiting to see if activity returns.

Do I need to clean before you arrive?

Washing and hot-drying bedding and clothing beforehand helps, but avoid deep-cleaning furniture or moving items between rooms, since that can spread bugs to areas that weren't affected.
