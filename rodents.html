Why supermarket traps aren't enough

Because rats and mice are naturally wary of anything new in their environment.

Rats in particular are neophobic - they avoid new objects, including traps and bait stations, for several days before they'll approach them, so a single trap set out once rarely catches more than the odd curious individual. Meanwhile a breeding pair of mice can produce dozens of offspring within a few months, and both species will gnaw through wiring, piping and insulation in roof voids and wall cavities, creating a genuine fire and damage risk long before numbers look obvious. Our approach pairs entry-point exclusion with tamper-resistant bait stations, so the population is brought down at the source instead of just picking off whoever wanders into the kitchen.

Worth checking for

Signs You've Got More Than One Visitor

Droppings along skirtings or in cupboards

Small dark pellet-shaped droppings near food storage, under sinks or along skirting boards are usually the first clear sign of activity.

Gnaw marks on wood, wiring or packaging

Rodents' teeth grow continuously, so they gnaw constantly to keep them worn down - chewed food packaging, skirting or cabling is a strong indicator.

Greasy rub marks on walls

Repeated travel along the same route leaves dark smudges where oily fur brushes against skirtings, pipes and wall corners.

Scratching or scurrying at night

Rodents are mostly active after dark, so noises in the roof void or wall cavities overnight often mean an established nest nearby.

Good to know

Rodent Control FAQs

Is the treatment safe for pest and children?

Disclaimer - bait is placed inside lockable, tamper-resistant stations rather than left out in the open, so treatment is safe for occupied homes with children and pets, as long as they cannot access the bait.

How soon will I see results?

Treatment usually reduces visible activity within the first week. Because rats and mice are cautious around anything new, full resolution through baiting typically takes 2–3 weeks as they gradually approach the stations.

Do I need to clean before you arrive?

No deep clean is needed, but clearing access to the roof hatch and any cluttered storage areas helps us inspect and treat efficiently.
