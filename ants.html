Why the supermarket spray isn't working

Because you're only treating the ants that already left home.

A visible trail is a scouting party, not the problem. The nest behind it can hold thousands of ants, and most household sprays only kill on contact - so you clear a counter for an afternoon while the colony carries on, and often splits into two colonies out of self-preservation, which is how a small line under the kettle turns into an infestation in two rooms. Our approach uses slow-acting baits that the workers carry back to the nest themselves, as well as targeted residual treatment at entry points, so the colony collapses from the inside instead of just scattering.

Worth checking for

Signs You've Got More Than a Few Stragglers

Trails that reappear in the same spot

Wiping it away and seeing the same line back within a day means there's an established scent trail to a nearby nest, not a random wanderer.

Activity near moisture

Ants cluster around sinks, dishwashers, and bathroom skirtings because colonies need a reliable water source close to the nest.

Small dirt-like mounds outdoors or indoors

Fine soil piles near paving cracks, foundations, or garden beds are often nest entrances - usually the source feeding the indoor trail.

Winged ants indoors

These are reproductive swarmers. Seeing them inside can mean an existing colony is mature enough to be expanding.

Good to know

Ant Control FAQs

Is the treatment safe for kids and pets?

Yes - we use products and application methods that are safe for occupied homes with children and pets, and we'll always tell you if any brief precaution is needed for a specific product.

How soon will I see results?

Most households notice a sharp drop in visible activity within 3–5 days, as bait works through the colony. Full resolution typically follows within two weeks.

Will one treatment be enough?

Often yes for an isolated indoor colony. For recurring activity - common in homes near gardens or complexes - our quarterly maintenance plan keeps colonies from re-establishing season after season.

Do I need to clean before you arrive?

No special prep needed. In fact, avoid wiping down trails right before your appointment - the scent trail actually helps us confirm where activity is concentrated.
