# Pest Control in Centurion

Treatment planned for freestanding homes, security estates, sectional-title complexes, offices and commercial properties.

A local, property-first approach

## Complexes and estates change how pests move

In Centurion, the place where a pest appears is not always where the problem starts. Cockroaches can travel along shared service routes, rodents may cross boundary walls and roof lines, and ants can nest outdoors before entering several kitchens.

A useful inspection therefore considers the affected unit together with drains, refuse areas, garages, gardens, roof voids and adjoining spaces where relevant. Treating one room repeatedly without checking those connections can leave the original pressure untouched.

Centurion treatment priorities

## Match the response to the property

### Follow harbourage and access

When ants, cockroaches or rodents are active, the first task is to identify where they shelter and enter. Treatment and aftercare should follow those findings.

### Coordinate termite protection early

Open soil, service penetrations and later disturbance matter. Construction teams should arrange treatment before relevant areas are permanently covered.

### Plan around staff and customers

Occupied sites may need discreet timing, routine monitoring, clear service records and escalation rules for urgent sightings.
