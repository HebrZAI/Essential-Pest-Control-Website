Why this matters more on a build

On a construction site, a pest problem is a program problem.

Your build has a schedule, a budget, and a warranty obligation attached to it long after the keys are handed over. Here's what's actually at stake if pest control gets treated as an afterthought.

Structural warranty claims

Undetected subterranean termite activity is one of the most common causes of post-handover structural disputes - and a claim traced back to missing or incomplete soil treatment lands squarely on the developer.

Stalled sign-off & enrolment

NHBRC enrolment and municipal sign-off both expect evidence of soil poisoning before foundations are covered. Missing that window means retroactive treatment, delays, and awkward conversations with your project manager.

Site safety incidents

Rubble piles, cleared land and open trenches on Gauteng's outlying developments are exactly where snakes, wasps and rodents relocate to - a bite or sting on site is a safety incident and a potential work stoppage.

What we're actually watching for

The Pests That Target Construction & Development Sites

01

Subterranean termites

Working undetected through soil and timber, they're the single biggest reason soil treatment before slab pour is non-negotiable - damage found after handover is far costlier than prevention before it.

02

Snakes

Cleared land, rubble piles and disturbed ground push snakes toward site offices and material stacks - especially on greenfield developments on the edges of Gauteng.

03

Mosquitoes

Open excavations, uncovered water tanks and rain-filled trenches are ideal breeding sites - a health risk to your crew that a filled-in hole doesn't fully solve.

04

Rodents

Site offices, stockpiled insulation and worker lunch waste give rats and mice everything they need to settle in long before the building itself has walls.

05

Wasps & bees

Scaffolding, roof cavities and dormant phases between build stages are exactly when colonies establish unnoticed - then surprise a crew coming back to finish the job.

06

Flies

Portable ablutions, waste skips and food waste at a busy site draw flies fast - a nuisance for the crew and a poor impression for site visits and inspections.

Questions we hear from contractors & developers

Construction & Development Pest Control FAQs

Do you provide a termite certificate for NHBRC enrolment?

Yes - soil treatment is documented with a certificate formatted for NHBRC enrolment and municipal sign-off, issued per stand or unit on multi-unit sites.

Can you treat before the foundation is poured?

Yes, and we recommend it. Soil treatment is far more effective - and far less disruptive - when applied to the prepared foundation area before the slab goes down, rather than after.

Do you work with phased or multi-unit developments?

Yes. Estates, townhouse complexes and sectional title builds get a phased schedule that tracks alongside your build program, with certificates issued per stand as each phase completes.

What happens if we find a snake or wasp nest on site?

Contact us and we'll prioritise a fast callout. Safety incidents on an active site aren't something we let sit until a routine visit - the goal is getting your crew back to work safely.

How long does termite soil treatment last?

Treatment is applied to give long-term protection appropriate to the soil type and foundation, and every certificate states the coverage period so it's clear for warranty and resale purposes.

Do you service greenfield sites outside the main metros?

Yes - we cover developments across Gauteng, including outlying and greenfield sites, and can schedule around access and site-readiness on newer builds.
