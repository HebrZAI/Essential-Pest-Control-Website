Why commercial clients choose us

Fully licensed. Fully accountable.

Pest control touches food safety, health and property - so we hold ourselves to the regulatory standards & protocols as set out by the Department of Agriculture, Forestry & Fisheries.

Audit-Ready Service & Treatment Logs

Every visit is logged with the product, dosage and area treated, and issued as a Certificate of Compliance - ready for your next health or safety audit.

Registered & Licensed PCOs

Our technicians are registered, fully licensed Pest Control Operators, holding valid certification to purchase, transport and apply regulated pesticides in South Africa.

Scheduled After-Hours & Weekend Call-Outs

We work around your shifts, trading hours and compliance calendar - not the other way around, with contracts tailored to how your site actually operates.
