Shared buildings need shared context

High-density properties require coordination

In an apartment block, office park or mixed-use building, pest activity may cross spaces controlled by different people. A tenant sees the cockroach, facilities manage the service duct, cleaning handles waste and security controls contractor access. A good response brings those pieces together.

We plan around the affected area, access rules, operating hours and need for discretion. Where evidence suggests movement through shared infrastructure, we may recommend a wider inspection rather than repeatedly treating the room where the complaint was logged.

Sandton questions

Discreet service and building access in Sandton

Can treatment be scheduled outside trading hours?

Often, yes. Availability depends on the site and treatment. Tell us the operating hours, access rules and areas involved so the visit can be planned with minimal disruption.

Will neighbouring apartments need treatment?

Not in every case. Adjoining units may need inspection when cockroaches, bed bugs or rodents could be moving through shared routes. The evidence and building layout determine the sensible scope.

Do you provide service records for facilities teams?

Yes. Discuss the level of reporting, monitoring and follow-up required when requesting a quote. An ongoing programme can be structured around the site’s operational needs.

What should a hotel do after a suspected bed bug report?

Limit unnecessary movement of linen, luggage and furniture, record where evidence was found and arrange identification promptly. Avoid moving items into another occupied room before assessment.
