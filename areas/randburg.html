Indoor signs can have outdoor causes

Older roofs and mature gardens create connected pest routes

Randburg’s mix of established houses, leafy gardens, townhouse complexes and retail strips means indoor activity often has an outdoor connection. A rodent heard above a bedroom may be entering near a tree or service cable. Ants in a kitchen may be nesting under paving.

We examine the immediate complaint and the conditions around it. This helps separate a one-off intruder from an established problem and identifies practical changes that support treatment instead of relying on repeated surface applications.

Randburg questions

What Randburg property owners should know

Can trees give rats access to my roof?

Branches near a roof can be one possible route, but rodents may also use walls, drainpipes, cables, damaged eaves and gaps around services. An inspection should consider all likely access points.

Why do ants return after I clean the trail?

Cleaning removes visible workers and their scent trail but may not affect the nest. Food, moisture, outdoor nesting sites and small entry gaps can support renewed activity.

Should I disturb a suspected termite mud tube?

Avoid unnecessary disturbance before identification. Photograph the tube and surrounding area, note whether insects are present and arrange an assessment.
