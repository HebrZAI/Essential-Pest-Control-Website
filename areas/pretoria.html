Pretoria questions

Planning pest control across Pretoria

Do you cover Pretoria beyond the eastern suburbs?

Yes. We receive enquiries across the wider Pretoria area. Send the suburb or a location pin when booking so the route and appointment availability can be confirmed.

Can one visit cover a home and outside buildings?

It can, provided the full scope is explained when the quote is requested. Mention garages, Wendy houses, storerooms, roof spaces or garden structures where evidence has been found.

Should a flat complex inspect more than one unit?

Possibly. When activity appears linked to shared walls, plumbing, ducts, passages, refuse areas or roof spaces, adjoining units and common property may need to be considered.

What happens after the technician finishes?

The technician explains what was treated, relevant precautions, likely changes in activity and practical conditions that could contribute to recurrence. Follow-up depends on the pest and treatment plan.
