Operational sites require operational context

A home and a loading bay cannot use the same pest plan

Kempton Park enquiries range from household cockroaches and roof rodents to warehouses receiving goods through busy loading areas. Guest accommodation may be concerned about bed bugs and discretion, while food premises need fast action and a clear record of what was found.

We define the treatment scope around how the site functions. Deliveries, waste flow, stock movement, kitchens, roof access, guest rooms and neighbouring properties can each change where monitoring or treatment should be concentrated.

Kempton Park questions

For Kempton Park homes and operational sites

Can you service an operating warehouse?

Yes. We first need to understand operating hours, restricted areas, stock type, loading activity, food handling and safety requirements. The visit should work with the operation rather than obstruct it.

What information helps with a commercial quote?

Provide the site type, approximate size, pest history, affected zones, hours, current monitoring, documentation needs and whether the request is urgent or planned.

Do bed bug bites confirm an infestation?

No. Skin reactions have several possible causes. Look for physical evidence such as live insects, cast skins, eggs or spotting and arrange identification before discarding furniture.
