Why the supermarket spray isn't working

Because cockroaches don't live where you spray - they live where you can't reach.

German cockroaches, the most common indoor species in Gauteng homes, shelter in tight, warm, moist gaps - behind fridges and stoves, inside cabinet hinges, under dishwashers - and a single female can be responsible for hundreds of offspring in a matter of months. Contact sprays kill those insects out foraging but do nothing for the egg cases and juveniles tucked away in the Colony, so the population rebuilds within weeks, sometimes days. Our approach combines targeted gel baiting at activity points with an insect growth regulator that breaks the breeding cycle, so the colony stops replacing itself instead of just thinning out temporarily.

Worth checking for

Signs You've Got More Than a Late-Night Straggler

A musty, oily smell in cupboards

Cockroaches release pheromones that build into a distinct musty odour in confined spaces like cabinets and pantries when a population is established.

Dark speck-like droppings

Small black or brown specks resembling ground coffee or pepper along skirtings and cabinet edges are a strong sign of regular activity nearby.

Egg cases in hidden corners

Small brown capsule-shaped egg cases tucked behind appliances or in drawer corners mean breeding is already underway, not just occasional visits.

Sightings in daylight

Roaches are naturally nocturnal, so spotting them moving around during the day often means the Colony is overcrowded and they're being pushed out to forage.

How we treat it

Our Cockroach Treatment Process

1

Inspection

We check likely activity - behind appliances, under sinks, cabinet voids and roof spaces - and identify the species, since German and American cockroaches respond differently to treatment.

2

Gel baiting at critical ativity points

Baits are placed directly into cracks, hinges and voids where roaches shelter, formulated to be carried back and shared through the population.

3

Growth regulator application

An insect growth regulator is applied to disrupt breeding, so juveniles can't mature and the population can't replace itself.

4

Report & prevention tips

You get a clear report on what we found, what was used, and simple habits - sealing entry points, managing moisture - that keep roaches from returning.

Good to know

Cockroach Control FAQs

Is the treatment safe for kids and pets?

Yes - gel baits are placed in cracks and voids out of reach rather than sprayed openly, so treatment is safe for occupied homes with children and pets. The spray that we use is also safe for humans and pets once dry.

How soon will I see results?

Most households notice a sharp drop in visible activity within the first week. Because egg cases hatch on a delay, full resolution typically takes 2–4 weeks as later generations pick up the bait.

Will one treatment be enough?

Often yes for an isolated indoor infestation. For recurring activity - common in complexes, older buildings, or homes near shared refuse areas - our quarterly maintenance plan keeps populations from re-establishing.

Do I need to clean before you arrive?

A light tidy of cupboards and under-sink areas helps us access points of activity, but there's no need for a deep clean - and please don't spray your own insecticide beforehand, as it can make roaches avoid our bait.
