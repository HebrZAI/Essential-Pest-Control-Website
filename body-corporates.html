A property-wide responsibility

One Complaint Often Points to a Bigger Pattern

Treating a single visible pest may quiet the complaint, but it rarely addresses shared entry points, service ducts, refuse areas or neighbouring units.

Resident complaints escalate quickly

Slow or unclear responses create frustration for residents, trustees and managing agents. We provide a practical action plan and clear feedback after service.

Pests move through shared infrastructure

Pipe runs, roof voids, drains, ducts and adjoining walls allow activity to spread. Effective control must consider the building, not only the reported unit.

Unplanned call-outs cost more

Repeated emergency treatments consume time and levy funds. Planned monitoring helps identify recurring risks before they become scheme-wide problems.

Where we focus

Protection Across Common Areas and Individual Units

01

Refuse rooms & bin areas

High-pressure zones for cockroaches, flies and rodents, especially where waste collection and cleaning schedules leave short windows for activity.

02

Gardens, boundaries & parking

We inspect perimeter walls, landscaped areas, stormwater routes, parking structures and service yards for nesting and entry points.

03

Kitchens, bathrooms & units

Targeted unit treatments are coordinated discreetly, with preparation instructions supplied in advance where occupant access is required.

04

Roof voids & service ducts

Shared voids can conceal rodents, cockroaches and other activity. These areas are assessed as part of the wider movement pattern.

05

Clubhouses & shared facilities

Communal kitchens, braai areas, laundries, gyms and pool surrounds require treatments suited to frequent resident use.

06

Vacant and changeover units

Inspections between occupants can uncover hidden infestations early and help prevent new tenants or owners inheriting an avoidable problem.

Questions from property managers and trustees

Body Corporate Pest Control FAQs

Can you treat only one affected unit?

Yes, where the issue is genuinely isolated. If inspection suggests activity is moving through shared walls, ducts, drains or neighbouring units, we will explain why a broader scope is likely to produce a more reliable result.

Do all residents need to leave during treatment?

Not usually. Access and re-entry requirements depend on the pest, product and treatment method. We provide clear instructions for affected residents before the appointment.

Can you work directly with residents to arrange access?

We can support coordinated access, but the preferred communication process is agreed with the property manager or body corporate first so responsibilities remain clear.

Do you offer recurring contracts?

Yes. Planned programs can cover common areas at an agreed frequency, with additional unit treatments or call-outs handled according to the service arrangement.

Can you service several properties under one account?

Yes. We can create site-specific plans and reporting while maintaining a consistent contact and service structure for a Gauteng property portfolio.

Which areas do you service?

We service body corporates, residential complexes, estates and managed properties across our Gauteng operating area. Contact us with the property location and we will confirm availability.
