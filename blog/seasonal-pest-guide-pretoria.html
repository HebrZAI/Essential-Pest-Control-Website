A Homeowner's Seasonal Pest Guide for Pretoria

A simple guide to the pests you may notice as the weather changes, the warning signs worth checking and the steps that can prevent a small problem from growing.

Pest activity changes through the year. Spring rain can bring termites and ants into view, summer heat helps cockroaches and flies multiply, and colder nights can send rats and mice looking for shelter.

This guide explains what Pretoria homeowners are most likely to notice in each season. It also gives you practical checks you can do around the house before pests become established.

Why Pest Activity Changes Through the Year

Warmth, rain, food and shelter affect pests in different ways. Termites and ants become more visible when the soil warms and rain adds moisture. Mosquitoes need standing water to breed. Rats and mice are more likely to enter buildings when outdoor temperatures fall.

The timing is not exact every year, but the pattern is useful. Checking the right places before each season can help you spot early activity and remove the conditions pests need.

Spring

September – November
Subterranean termites Ants Early mosquitoes Wasps

Warm days and the first good rains make spring an important time to watch for termites. Winged termites may gather around lights or windows, and discarded wings may appear on floors or windowsills. This does not automatically mean timber is being damaged inside the house, but it is a good reason to arrange an inspection.

Ant trails also become more noticeable as the soil warms. Rainwater left in gutters, buckets and plant saucers can support mosquitoes, while wasps may begin building small nests under eaves.

Spring checklist

  • After rain, look for narrow mud tubes on foundations, walls and wooden posts.
  • Photograph winged insects or discarded wings so they can be identified.
  • Clear leaves from gutters and empty standing water each week.
  • Trim plants that touch exterior walls and keep soil or mulch below the damp-proof course.
  • Arrange a termite inspection if you find mud tubes, damaged timber or repeated swarms.

Summer

December – February
Cockroaches Mosquitoes Flies Wasps & bees Ants

Heat and moisture allow many insects to breed faster. Cockroaches are drawn to warm, damp areas near kitchens, bathrooms, drains and appliances. Mosquito numbers rise where rainwater stands, while flies breed around bins, pet waste and other organic material.

Ants may enter for food and water, and wasp or bee nests that started in spring can be larger by late summer. Do not disturb an active nest if anyone nearby could be allergic to stings.

Summer checklist

  • Empty and scrub birdbaths, trays and water containers weekly.
  • Keep bins closed and rinse food containers before recycling.
  • Store dry food in sealed containers and clean crumbs and grease promptly.
  • Repair dripping taps, leaking pipes and blocked drains.
  • Watch eaves for bee or wasp traffic and get advice before approaching a nest.

Autumn

March – May
Rodents (early movement) Spiders Ants nesting in

Autumn is the best time to prepare for rodents. As nights cool, rats and mice begin testing gaps around roofs, garages, doors and service pipes. Closing entry points before they settle indoors is usually easier than dealing with an established nest.

You may also notice more spiders inside as they follow insects or find sheltered areas. A sudden increase in spiders can point to another insect problem nearby.

Autumn checklist

  • Check roof edges and openings around pipes, cables and vents.
  • Repair worn door seals and damaged vent covers.
  • Keep firewood raised off the ground and away from exterior walls.
  • Reduce clutter in garages and storerooms.
  • Investigate scratching sounds, droppings or gnaw marks early.

Winter

June – August
Rodents (peak season) Cockroaches (indoors) Bed bugs

Rodent complaints often rise in winter because homes provide warmth, food and nesting material. Common signs include scratching in ceilings at night, droppings in cupboards or garages, gnawed packaging and damaged wiring.

Cockroaches can remain active indoors around warm appliances, drains and damp cupboards. Bed bugs are not seasonal; they can be introduced at any time through luggage, furniture or other belongings.

Winter checklist

  • Check pantry corners, under sinks and garage storage for droppings or gnaw marks.
  • Store pet food and bird seed in strong, sealed containers.
  • Inspect mattress seams after travel or before bringing second-hand furniture inside.
  • Seal gaps only after confirming rodents are not trapped inside the structure.
  • Get professional help if activity continues or rodents are inside a ceiling or wall cavity.

Pests That Don't Take a Season Off

Some pests depend more on access to people, food and indoor shelter than on outdoor weather. They can appear in any month.

Pest Main trigger Why it's not seasonal
Bed bugs Travel, second-hand furniture, shared laundry Indoor temperatures stay stable year-round, so they spread through movement of people and belongings rather than weather.
German cockroach Food access, clutter, moisture Lives mainly indoors near steady sources of heat, food and moisture.
Pantry moths & weevils Stored food, packaging Arrive in purchased goods and breed inside sealed storage regardless of the month.

Local Areas and Property Types

Your property's age, garden, plumbing and surroundings usually matter more than its postcode. These are useful local examples rather than guarantees.

Pretoria East - Moreleta Park, Faerie Glen, Garsfontein, Equestria, Silver Lakes

Established gardens, irrigation and timber features can create suitable conditions for ants and termites. Check areas where soil, paving or planting sits close to the building.

Centurion - Hennopspark, Wierdapark, Rooihuiskraal, Irene

Homes near waterways, undeveloped land or dense vegetation may experience more rodent movement. Standing water and irrigation problems can also increase mosquito activity.

Pretoria North - Montana, Wonderboom, Sinoville

Both older and newer homes can have openings around roof edges, vents and services. An autumn entry-point check is worthwhile before colder weather arrives.

Brooklyn, Menlyn, Hatfield

Flats, offices and other multi-unit buildings can share plumbing, service ducts and wall spaces. Cockroach or rodent treatment works best when connected areas are inspected together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do termites swarm in Pretoria in spring?

Warm weather and the first good rains can trigger established colonies to release winged termites. They often gather near outdoor lights or windows during September and October.

Why do I get more rodents in winter?

Colder nights encourage rats and mice to seek warmth, food and shelter in roof spaces, garages and wall cavities. Small gaps around roofs, doors, pipes and cables can provide access.

How often should a Pretoria home be inspected for pests?

There is no single schedule for every home. Seasonal checks are useful, while a property with previous termite activity may benefit from a professional termite inspection each year.

Can I treat seasonal pests myself, or should I call a professional?

Housekeeping, removing standing water and routine maintenance are sensible DIY prevention steps. Get professional advice for termites, recurring cockroaches, rodents inside the structure, or bee and wasp nests where safe removal requires specialist care.
