Compliance you can audit

Fully licensed. Fully accountable.

Pest control touches food safety, health and property - so we hold ourselves to the regulatory standards & protocols as set out by the Department of Agriculture, Forestry & Fisheries

Compliance Certifications For The Food Industry

Our treatment protocols in food-handling, restaurants and commercial kitchen environments are aligned with the strict protocols as set forth by the Department of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries. We do inspections, treatments and issue a Certificate of Compliance recognised by The Department of Health. This is done on a 3 monthly cycle.

Registered & Licensed PCO

Our technicians are registered, fully licensed Pest Control Operators, holding valid certification to purchase, transport and apply regulated pesticides in South Africa.

Documented & Insured

Every job is logged with product, dosage and area treated - and every technician on site is covered by full public liability insurance.
