Areas We Inspect in Offices and Retail Premises

01

Reception, sales floors & displays

Customer-facing zones require discreet inspection and treatment, with attention to doors, display units, counters and hidden edges.

02

Staff kitchens & pause areas

Food, appliances, cupboards and waste create concentrated pressure for cockroaches, ants, flies and rodents.

03

Stockrooms & filing areas

Cartons, archived paperwork, stored products and low-disturbance corners provide shelter and make early evidence easy to overlook.

04

Ablutions, drains & service ducts

Moisture and shared plumbing routes can support cockroach or fly activity and connect different parts of a multi-tenant building.

05

Deliveries, rear doors & waste

Goods entrances, bin areas and service passages are common entry and feeding points away from the polished customer-facing space.

06

Ceilings, roof spaces & perimeters

Rodents and birds may use roof voids, vegetation, parking areas and building gaps before activity becomes visible indoors.

Office & Retail Pest Control FAQs

Can you treat after the office or store has closed?

Yes, where scheduling and the selected treatment allow it. We can arrange a suitable service window with the nominated site contact and provide any re-entry instructions in advance.

Will staff or customers need to leave?

That depends on the pest, treatment method and affected zone. Some targeted work causes minimal interruption, while other treatments require temporary access restrictions. We clarify this before service.

What if the pest is coming from another tenant?

We can treat the affected premises, but lasting control may require inspection or action in shared areas or neighbouring units. We document the likely movement route for building management.

Can you provide recurring preventative servicing?

Yes. The recommended frequency depends on the premises, pest pressure, food and waste activity, delivery patterns and previous history.

Can you service several branches?

Yes. We can build site-specific scopes while keeping communication and reporting consistent across multiple Gauteng offices or retail branches.

What should we do when a pest is seen during trading hours?

Record the exact location and time, remove exposed food or vulnerable stock where relevant, and contact us. Avoid broad DIY spraying because it may scatter activity or make the source harder to identify.
